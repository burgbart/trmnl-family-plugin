trmnl-home Design System

Design language for every non-TRMNL view — web pages, widgets, desktop and AI surfaces. Based on the friendly, modern SaaS look of heimdalsecurity.com: deep navy, vivid pink CTAs, blue gradients, generous rounding.

Motion & animation

Motion is subtle and functional — short, ease-out, never decorative on data views. Three durations (150/250/400ms), three easings (standard, entrance, exit), one default entrance (fade-up). Always honor prefers-reduced-motion — this page disables all animation when the OS asks for it.

fade-up entrance
400ms · easing.entrance
(replay by reloading)
hover lift
150ms · easing.standard
buttons, cards
slow hover lift
400ms · too heavy for
small elements — compare

Rule of thumb: micro-interactions (hover, color) use duration.fast; state changes (toggles, tabs) use duration.normal; entrances and modals use duration.slow with easing.entrance. Nothing loops, nothing bounces.

Gradient usage

Two gradients exist — gradient.cta (pink) and gradient.blue — and they have strict jobs. Pink means "act", blue means "decorate".

Do

Primary CTA

The pink gradient is reserved for the one primary action per view.

Get started
Do

Icon chips & decorative art

The blue gradient fills circular icon chips, hero illustrations, and chart accents.

Don't

Behind body copy

Gradients never sit under paragraphs or lists — contrast and readability suffer.

Hard to read, never do this.
Don't

Multiple gradients in one view

One gradient element per screen region. A pink CTA plus blue icon chips is fine; competing gradient panels are not.
